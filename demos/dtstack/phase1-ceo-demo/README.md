# CEO Group Phase 1 Management Cockpit Demo

This interactive prototype demonstrates a focused Phase 1 management reporting experience for CEO Group. All users, figures, contracts and project records are fictional and are provided for demonstration purposes only.

## Business Objectives

The demo focuses on two high-priority management domains:

1. **Financial Status**
2. **Real Estate Operations — Sales and Leasing**

Its primary objectives are to:

- Provide executives with a concise and consistent view of current business performance.
- Replace fragmented periodic reports with role-specific management dashboards.
- Highlight financial and commercial exceptions that require management attention.
- Trace headline indicators down to supporting contracts or projects.
- Generate a concise management report directly from the active dashboard.
- Provide a responsive experience for laptops, tablets and smartphones.

## Role Dashboards

### CEO

The CEO receives two group-level dashboards.

**Executive Finance** covers:

- Operating cash flow and cash movement.
- Receivables aging and overdue exposure.
- Revenue by business unit.
- Gross-margin performance by region.

**Executive Real Estate** covers:

- Sales absorption by project.
- Unsold inventory value.
- Regional sales performance.
- Leasing occupancy by asset type.
- Project-level commercial performance.

### Deputy CEO

The Deputy CEO receives the **Operations Control Dashboard**, focused on:

- Operating and collection exceptions.
- Regional execution performance.
- Project execution status.
- Action owners and deadlines.
- Items requiring cross-functional management intervention.

### Sales Director

The Sales Director receives the **Sales & Leasing Dashboard**, focused on:

- Assigned-region sales performance.
- Commercial funnel conversion.
- Unsold commercial inventory.
- Broker conversion.
- Project-level sales priorities and next actions.

## Interaction Overview

### Simulated Login and Role Switching

The opening screen provides dropdowns for selecting a demo user and dashboard role. The selected role determines which management dashboard is displayed. The user control in the top-right corner reopens the login screen for switching roles during the demonstration.

### Drill-Down

Drill-down indicators are highlighted with blue borders, a **DRILL DOWN** label and a persistent **View details** action.

The main drill-down examples are:

- **Overdue receivables:** opens the contracts contributing to receivables overdue by more than 60 days.
- **Unsold inventory:** opens the projects contributing to total unsold inventory value.

### Management Report Generation

The **Generate report** button creates a concise report based on the active role dashboard. The report includes:

- An executive summary.
- Three headline metrics.
- Key management findings.
- Recommended actions.
- Current dashboard and reporting context.

The generated report can be printed or saved as a PDF through the browser's print dialog.

### Responsive and Language Experience

The login screen, dashboards, KPI cards, tables, drill-down dialogs and reports adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile layouts. The language control switches the presentation between English and Vietnamese.

## Core Demo Flow

1. Log in as the **CEO**.
2. Review the **Executive Finance** dashboard.
3. Click the overdue-receivables KPI or aging chart to open the contract drill-down.
4. Switch to the **Executive Real Estate** dashboard.
5. Click the unsold-inventory KPI or chart to open the project drill-down.
6. Generate a management report from the current CEO dashboard.
7. Switch to the **Deputy CEO** to demonstrate the Operations Control Dashboard and action tracking.
8. Switch to the **Sales Director** to demonstrate the commercial funnel, sales performance and inventory priorities.
9. Open the demo on a tablet or smartphone to demonstrate responsive behavior.
